The NRT years come from a different satellite record (VIIRS) than GFED5.1 (MODIS). They are the best continuation available, but a jump at 2023 can partly be a change of instrument. They are shaded in the yearly charts.

Land-cover groups

GroupGFED5.1 classes

Forest overall = forest + deforestation fires. Forest without deforestation and ag fires = forest only. Agricultural fires are GFED's cropland class and are never counted as forest.

Trends

The trend is the Theil–Sen slope, the median of the slopes between every pair of years, so one extreme fire season barely moves it. It is shown as % of the period's mean per year, so large and small places compare. Mann–Kendall (normal approximation) tests whether the rise or fall could be chance; p < 0.05 counts as significant. With 20–25 years of noisy fire data, many real changes do not pass.

Countries and regions

Regions are GFED's 14 basis regions, a clean split of the grid. Countries come from Natural Earth 1:50m boundaries; a cell crossed by a border is shared by land area at 0.05°. About 99% of fire carbon lands inside a country; the rest falls on coasts and small islands.

Limits

  • GFED emissions include soil and peat burning; other products that count only above-ground biomass are lower, most of all in the boreal zone.
  • The 0.25° grid cannot resolve small countries; they are greyed out by default.
  • Each cell is split into classes by GFED's own land-cover maps; another map would draw the forest/savanna line differently.
