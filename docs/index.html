About the data

1997–2001: GFED5.1 total carbon only. 2002–2022: GFED5.1 with 16 land-cover classes. 2023–2025: GFED5.1 NRT (VIIRS), same classes; a step at 2023 can partly be the instrument change.

Maps: 0.25° GFED cells (about 28 km). Unusual year = that year minus the cell's 2002–2025 mean. Trend map = per 1° cell, 2002–2025, Theil–Sen with Mann–Kendall; faded cells are not significant.

Forest = sparse boreal, boreal, temperate and tropical forest classes; forest overall adds deforestation fires; agricultural fires are GFED's cropland class. Biomes are GFED's 14 basis regions. Border cells are shared between countries by land area at 0.05°.
